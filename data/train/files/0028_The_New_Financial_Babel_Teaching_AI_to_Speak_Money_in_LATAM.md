# The New Financial Babel: Teaching AI to Speak Money in LATAM

Источник: https://medium.com/mercadolibre-tech/la-nueva-babel-financiera-ense%C3%B1ar-a-la-ia-a-hablar-dinero-en-latinoam%C3%A9rica-4605235e3aac

Every financial transaction tells a story — from your morning coffee to your monthly subscriptions. But until now, these stories remained scattered and untold. Imagine a world where all your transactions aren’t just numbers, but pieces of a personalized narrative powered by Artificial Intelligence. Thanks to Open Finance — which allows users to voluntarily share their financial data with authorized third parties — those once-isolated stories locked away in banking vaults are now becoming part of an open, interconnected library, always with the right permissions. In this article, we’ll show you how at MELI we’re decoding this new financial ‘language’ using GenAI, tackling LATAM’s unique challenges to turn raw data into real value.
How do you unify the story of countless transactions written in different “dialects” — across formats, contexts, and points in time?
To answer this, we’ll walk you through MELI’s journey: from the early chaos of manual rules to the transformative power of GenAI. We’ll show you the evolution of our approach detailing how we use Large Language Models (LLMs) and custom-trained semantic embeddings to transform Financial Data Enrichment — the process of turning raw transaction data into structured insights, see Figure 1 — from a slow, manual task into a fast, orchestrated engine for innovation, all built upon our core data infrastructure, including Fury and DataMesh. By the end, you’ll understand how this shift from scattered data to a shared financial language will change your relationship with your money.
📖 The challenge of understanding LATAM’s new financial dialect
The need to turn financial data into meaningful knowledge isn’t unique to MELI. Across Latin America, the financial ecosystem is rapidly evolving: Brazil, currently in Phase 4 of its Open Finance implementation — focusing on integrating a broader range of financial products such as investments, insurance, etc. — leads the way, while countries like Colombia, Mexico, and Chile are forging their own paths toward Open Finance. With financial data now unlocked, access is no longer the main barrier — the real challenge lies in interpreting and structuring that data efficiently. At MELI, this reality pushed us to build highly adaptable solutions.
Let’s dive into one of those solutions and see how we turned financial hieroglyphs into a new common language, thanks to GenAI, Open Finance, and a little MELI magic.
🚧 How we turned financial hieroglyphs into a shared language
Let’s say you already have access to this financial library and the secure infrastructure to manage it (remember, we’re talking about highly regulated systems here). The question is: how do you extract meaningful context from all that data and turn it into something useful?
📜 The early chaos: The manual rules era
Picture this: it’s 2021, and you’ve just stepped into a massive financial library filled with terabytes of data — like scrolls written in different dialects, scattered without rhyme or reason. Back then, categorizing millions of transactions relied heavily on handcrafted rules based on regular expressions (regex) tailored for each country, and MCC (Merchant Category Codes) which, being manually reported, often led to wildly inaccurate categorizations. (And don’t even get us started on how thrilled stakeholders were every time they requested changes 😂.)
This approach caused frequent inconsistencies such as classifying a “Posto de Gasolina” (gas station) under “8220-Educational Services”. It also demanded constant tweaks for each country and created a huge barrier to scale due to local language nuances, like ‘birreria’ vs. ‘cervecería’ 🍻.
Even something seemingly simple like classifying “Digital Subscriptions” required hundreds of lines of regex that had to be manually updated if popular services like Spotify, Netflix, or Mercado Libre changed their naming conventions even slightly. The work was titanic, artisanal, expensive — and totally unsustainable given the daily tsunami of new data.
We barely managed to build our first model using this method, focused only on debit transactions in Portuguese 🥹 (you may have seen it in “Seu Dinheiro” section of Mercado Pago). But this artisanal system, built on a fragile foundation of regex, was a nightmare to maintain. We hadn’t just hit a wall; we were facing a tsunami of data that our approach simply couldn’t handle. It was obvious we needed a truly intelligent solution. That’s when we turned to GenAI.
🔍 The arrival of GenAI: A smart financial translator
Fast forward to late-2022: you’ve just launched your first rule-based solution (with all the headaches we just covered 📜), and suddenly the GenAI/ChatGPT buzz hits the internet. Panic sets in:
Are all those months — maybe years — of work about to be obsolete? Can a single LLM really replace this painstaking system?
At the time, GPT-2 wasn’t even close to being useful for this kind of task. Plus, training your own specialized corpus (a meticulously labeled financial dataset) would cost a fortune 💰 and raise serious privacy concerns. GPT-3 looked promising but was still prohibitively expensive and lacked a public API. But the landscape changed faster than you could create a new category manually.
Enter March 2023: GPT-3.5 Turbo arrives — cost-efficient and accessible through a public API. Suddenly, what once felt like an educated guess became a model that could deliver over 80% accuracy with the right setup, far surpassing our regex-based approach (which hovered around 60%). We also slashed operational costs (down 75%) and scaled up transaction categorization: from tens of millions per quarter to tens of millions per week.
Of course, it took some magic — optimizing API usage with batch processing before it was cool, mastering concepts like temperature and context windows, and using a bit of Python with Fury to orchestrate it all. That initial panic became our catalyst for innovation. At MELI, we thrive on that kind of adrenaline, so we embraced the change and orchestrated our large-scale enrichment workflows from end to end assisted by AI and validated by humans. The result? It was like putting a turbocharger on our Financial Data Enricher.
💡 Technical Insight:
The real game-changer wasn’t just using an LLM — it was designing a human-in-the-loop labeling workflow to scale without sacrificing control or quality.
We built a feedback loop combining GenAI prompts with human validation, creating clear semantic patterns that freed us from manual processing. (stakeholders were very happy 😂).
Every GenAI-generated model and decision at MELI goes through a continuous validation process, combining LLM power with expert human feedback. This ensures precision, traceability, and control in a world where data quality is key to real decisions impacting millions of users — always with a strong commitment to ethics, regulation, and privacy.
This LLM-assisted, human-validated system marked a significant leap forward, dramatically improving accuracy and efficiency. However, as we continued to scale and push the boundaries of financial data enrichment, the inherent latency and cost challenges of relying solely on external LLM APIs for hundreds of millions of weekly transactions became apparent. This realization drove us to explore how we could internalize and optimize this semantic power even further.
🪬 Semantic embeddings: Building smart financial patterns
As 2024 began, things seemed stable. New models appear, but none significantly outperform the ones we’re using — until July, when GPT-4o-mini lands. It’s extremely cost-efficient (60% cheaper than GPT-3.5 Turbo) and delivers stronger performance. The fundamental principle of our solution was to build a flexible, model-agnostic architecture. This design proved its value by allowing us to efficiently integrate new advancements like GPT-4o-mini and experiment with other key players like Gemini and Claude.
This inherent adaptability is what allows our system to evolve with the AI landscape, integrating future advancements to maintain its long-term relevance.
LLM APIs were a massive breakthrough, but querying them for hundreds of millions of transactions presents long-term latency and cost challenges. That’s where embeddings come in: they distill semantic intelligence into a format that’s much faster and cheaper to operate in real time. What if we could transfer the advanced financial knowledge of these massive models into lighter, faster, and more affordable formats?

This challenge led us to explore semantic embeddings — numerical representations that capture deep relationships between words and concepts. For example, they recognize that “delivery” and “fast food” belong to the same context, even if they use different words. At MELI, we developed our own embeddings, fine-tuning BERT-style (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) models on regional financial text.
Thanks to this strategy, the results were remarkable: we boosted our global categorization accuracy from 80% to 90%, and further reduced operational costs by more than 30%.
We now automatically distinguish restaurants from grocery stores, pinpoint exact spending like meals, and instantly recognize that “Mercado Livre” in Brazil and “Mercado Libre” in Mexico belong to the same category, despite the language difference.
🚀 What we unlocked:
By distilling the intelligence of LLMs into locally trained financial embeddings, we enabled real-time responses with lower cost and greater semantic precision.
This solution didn’t just cut operating costs further — it now allows us to respond in near real time (NRT) to millions of transactions, maximizing the power and accuracy of the latest GenAI generation.
