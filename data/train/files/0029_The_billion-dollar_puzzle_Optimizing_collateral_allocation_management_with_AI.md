The billion-dollar puzzle: Optimizing collateral allocation management with AI
Imagine you have a collection of items, each with a specific weight and value. Your goal is to choose a combination of these items to maximize the total value without exceeding your bag’s weight limit. Once you’ve filled your bag, any remaining items must be left behind, so you want to ensure that the items you pack provide the greatest possible value. How would you tackle this challenge? There are numerous strategies to explore, but what would be the most effective approach to consistently achieve the best outcome?
This situation is similar to the daily challenge faced by the Collateral Management team at Mercado Pago. Collateral management involves the process through which banks and fintech companies efficiently allocate collateral assets to enhance risk management, liquidity, and capital utilization in financial transactions. Collateral refers to an asset that a borrower offers to a lender as security for a loan, protecting the lender against default risk.
To continue expanding its loans portfolio, Mercado Pago secures capital from the largest banks. These credit lines total billions of dollars and require backing from millions of loans within Mercado Pago’s portfolio. Each loan must comply with numerous conditions concerning risk ratings, amounts, terms, and other specific features. Therefore, the Collateral Management team must carefully select which loans to assign to each credit line.
To tackle this formidable challenge, we developed Enigma, a solution that automates and optimizes the loan selection process for securitizing each credit line.
Key aspects of complexity in collateral allocation management
The complexities of collateral allocation management at Mercado Pago stem from several key aspects:
- A time-constrained process: The daily collateral allocation process must be completed within a few hours, requiring swift decision-making.
- Vast loan portfolio: Our portfolio consists of millions of loans valued in billions of dollars, requiring careful management and selection.
- Multiple credit lines: Numerous credit lines need collateral, each with distinct requirements and characteristics.
- Complex contractual conditions: Each credit line comes with a variety of contractual conditions, which can lead to complex issues of overlap.
- Dynamic market conditions: The team must adapt to strategic conditions that fluctuate based on market trends.
- Regulatory compliance: Complying with various regulations adds another layer of complexity to the allocation process.
Optimizing allocations: The power of linear programming and OR-Tools
To achieve optimal allocation, we employed linear programming, a mathematical method used to optimize a function subject to constraints. This approach is particularly well-suited to our problem because it allows us to model an objective function to optimize (such as maximizing the allocated amount and minimizing funding cost), along with various constraints (including contractual and strategic conditions), using linear expressions. By applying linear programming, we can systematically evaluate potential allocations and ensure that all constraints are met while striving for the best possible outcome.
To facilitate this process, we used Google OR-Tools, an open-source optimization library developed by Google AI. OR-Tools enhances our linear programming efforts by enabling us to identify the best solution from a range of possibilities and integrates seamlessly with multiple solvers, both commercial and open-source. Designed to tackle a variety of optimization problems, OR-Tools is highly adaptable to our complex requirements, allowing us to explore numerous scenarios and quickly converge on the optimal allocation strategy tailored to our unique needs.
Navigating scalability: Grouping heuristic to optimize execution time
One of the primary challenges we encountered was the vast size of Mercado Pago’s loan portfolio, which includes millions of individual loans. This sheer volume introduced significant complexity in the selection process, causing an exponential increase in execution time as the portfolio size grew (as illustrated in Image 1). From a computational complexity perspective, this problem is NP-Complete (short for nondeterministic polynomial time complete), meaning that, while a solution can be verified quickly, there is no known way to find a solution quickly.
To address this issue, we developed an in-house grouping heuristic that enables us to strategically combine loans with similar characteristics into clusters. This technique effectively reduces the overall number of loan entries the solution must evaluate, streamlining the selection process. By using this grouping heuristic, we maintain the required execution time while preserving the overall performance of the solution. Moreover, this strategy allows us to effectively navigate the challenges posed by our large loan portfolio, facilitating a more efficient and responsive allocation algorithm.

Once the inputs are collected, they flow into the heart of the system: the Optimization Model. This model employs advanced optimization techniques to analyze the loans based on various criteria and constraints. The goal is to determine the most suitable loans to assign to each credit line to maximize or minimize the objective function while ensuring compliance with risk ratings, amounts, and other contractual conditions.
As the optimization process completes, Enigma generates outputs that include a refined selection of loans. These results facilitate comprehensive reporting and data visualization. Enigma’s integration within our in-house Fury infrastructure enhances its scalability and efficiency, enabling it to handle complex data processing tasks seamlessly.
